# JLPT N2 reading: the real format

Source: the official N2 sample test, `https://www.jlpt.jp/samples/sample2012/pdf/N2R.pdf`
and `N2answer.pdf`. Re-check there if anything looks off.

## The 5 types

| 大問 | Type | Texts × questions | Sample answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 問題10 | 内容理解（短文） | 5 texts × 1 | 3 2 2 1 2 |
| 問題11 | 内容理解（中文） | 3 texts × 3 | 3 4 4 / 3 1 4 / 3 2 1 |
| 問題12 | 統合理解 | 1 pair (A, B) × 2 | 1 3 |
| 問題13 | 主張理解（長文） | 1 text × 3 | 2 4 4 |
| 問題14 | 情報検索 | 1 flyer/table × 2 | 4 1 |

21 questions, all with 4 choices.

## Instructions (word for word)

- 問題10: 次の(1)から(5)の文章を読んで、後の問いに対する答えとして最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。
- 問題11: 次の(1)から(3)の文章を読んで、後の問いに対する答えとして最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。
- 問題12: 次のAとBはそれぞれ、〈topic〉について書かれた文章である。二つの文章を読んで、後の問いに対する答えとして最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。
- 問題13: 次の文章を読んで、後の問いに対する答えとして最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。
- 問題14: 右のページは、〈what it is〉である。下の問いに対する答えとして最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。

## What each type looks like

**短文 (about 200–230 characters).** Essay extracts (an opinion on reading, on what
makes a painting), or practical texts with a lead-in line (以下は、ある会社が出したメールの
内容である。) such as a business email in full keigo (お客様各位 … 弊社では … 幸いです).
Questions: 筆者の考えに合うものはどれか / 〜について正しいものはどれか /
<u>下線部</u>とあるが、なぜか / 筆者が考える〜とはどのようなものか.

**中文 (about 470–620 characters).** An essay that explains something (why fast running is
hard to learn: the nerve circuits are already fixed). Underlined parts are numbered ①②.
Typical question set: 1 × why ① (理由), 1 × what ② refers to (この場合とは
どんな場合か: 指示語), 1 × the writer's conclusion (筆者によると、〜にはどうすればよいか).

**統合理解 (two texts of about 300 characters each).** A and B on the same topic (the
future of cars), both reasonable, partly overlapping. Questions: AとBのどちらの文章にも
触れられている点は何か / AとBの筆者は、〜についてどのように考えているか. Choices for
the second are built as AもBも… / AもBも… / Aは…、Bは… / Aは…、Bは….

**主張理解 (about 850 characters).** A personal essay with an argument (why likes
and dislikes shouldn't drive research; the writer's own path into science). Questions:
why the writer holds the underlined view / a fact about the writer's experience /
筆者は、〜とはどのようなものだと考えているか (the main claim).

**情報検索 (a flyer, plan table or flowchart).** Two companies' moving plans: a table
with conditions (box count, who packs, price, notes like 「プラン④のみ、他のプランに
追加してのご利用が可能です」) and a yes/no flowchart. Each question is a person with 2–3
needs (10+ boxes, cheapest, but 5 urgent boxes fast); the answer combines plans and
depends on a footnote.

## How the wrong choices are built

From the sample's answer key:

- **Uses the text's words but changes one detail**: who, when, how much (5%引き vs
  5%引きより安く; プリンターを買う人 vs プリンターを使っている人).
- **Opposite of the text** (目の前にあるものをできるだけ現実に近づけて when the writer
  says copying what you see is never painting).
- **Adds something the text never says** (想像力で補い).
- **True in the text but not the answer to this question** (a reason given elsewhere, or
  the writer's example instead of the claim).
- **Over-general** (どんな研究であっても…).
- 情報検索: a plan that fits 2 of 3 needs, or misses the footnote.

Hard words get a gloss under the text: （注）購入する：買う. Choices never quote a long
stretch of the text verbatim; the right one paraphrases.

## Difficulty: measured from the official sample

Measured by line count (about 38 characters a line) on the sample pages.

| Type | Characters | （注） glosses | Source of the real text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 短文 | about 200–230 | 0–3 | essay extracts, a business email |
| 中文 | about 470–620 | 5 each | a newspaper column (日本経済新聞), a popular-science book |
| 統合理解 | about 270 each | 1 for the pair | two short opinion pieces |
| 主張理解 | about 850 | 5 | a book by a researcher (robotics, on likes and dislikes) |

**Where the answer is.** It's often far from the underline:
- 短文(4): the underline is the first sentence ("人に強い影響を与えるのは大部からなる作品とは限りません"),
  but the reason in the answer (読み手の姿勢で決まる) comes from the **last** sentence
  (学ぼうとする姿勢があれば…).
- 主張理解: the main claim is in the **last two paragraphs**, after a pivot question
  (ところで、そもそも好き嫌いとは何だろうか？). The first half is a lead-in that a reader can
  mistake for the claim.

**How far the right answer is paraphrased.** Usually no shared content words:
- 単なる偶然の積み重なりの結果なのだ → たまたまそうなったから
- 目の前の自然を心のなかに消化し、それをもう一度吐きだす → 目で見たものを心のなかに感じ取って描き表したもの
- 納得し、正当化するため（注: 間違っていなかったと思う） → 自分の行動や選択が間違っていなかったと思うために用いるもの

The **wrong** choices are the ones that reuse the text's words.

**The writing itself:**
- Claims are hedged: 〜のではないか, 〜と言えるのではないか, 〜のだろう, 〜のかもしれない, 〜ように思う.
  The reader has to tell the writer's view from the views the writer reports.
- Rhetorical questions carry the argument (なぜ安いのか。本来の価値は損なわれていないか。).
- Newspaper style uses short noun-ending sentences (不ぞろいな野菜は、むしろ手作り品を思わせる長所。).
- Vocabulary is N2 with a few N1-level words, all glossed: 肥えている, ぬくもり, 感性, 規格,
  粗悪品, 矯正, 糸口, 狭める, 得手不得手, 元来, 正当化, 普遍的, 理屈.
- Sentences run about 30–50 characters, with some long ones built on 〜が、〜し、〜ため.

## Checklist before showing a passage

Every passage must pass all of these, or be rewritten:

1. Length and glosses within the ranges above.
2. For essays (中文, 主張理解, most 短文): the writer's view is hedged and appears in the
   second half, not the first sentence; 主張理解 has a pivot before the claim.
3. At least one question per 中文/主張理解 passage needs a sentence **two or more
   sentences away** from the underline or question topic.
4. The right answer shares no key content word with the sentence that proves it
   (paraphrase), while at least one wrong answer reuses the text's words.
5. Every wrong answer has a named trap (changed detail, opposite, not in text, true but
   not the answer, over-general, missed condition), and none is defensible.
6. At least 2 glossed words above N2, and none of the gloss words appear in a choice.
7. 情報検索: each question needs 2+ conditions and at least one footnote or exception.

## What this means when writing passages

- Natural written Japanese at N2: である style for essays, full keigo for business
  emails and notices.
- Gloss words above N2 with （注）, as the real test does.
- Each question has exactly one choice that the text supports; for every wrong choice,
  know which trap above it uses (needed for the explanation).
- Spread answers over 1–4.
