# JLPT N2 reading: the real format

Source: the official N2 sample test, `https://www.jlpt.jp/samples/sample2012/pdf/N2R.pdf`
and `N2answer.pdf`. Re-check there if anything looks off.

## The 5 types

| 大問 | Type | Texts × questions | Sample answers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 問題10 | 内容理解（短文） | 5 texts × 1 | 3 2 2 1 2 |
| 問題11 | 内容理解（中文） | 3 texts × 3 | 3 4 4 / 3 1 4 / 3 2 1 |
| 問題12 | 統合理解 | 1 pair (A, B) × 2 | 1 3 |
| 問題13 | 主張理解（長文） | 1 text × 3 | 2 4 4 |
| 問題14 | 情報検索 | 1 flyer/table × 2 | 4 1 |

21 questions, all with 4 choices.

## Instructions (word for word)

- 問題10: 次の(1)から(5)の文章を読んで、後の問いに対する答えとして最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。
- 問題11: 次の(1)から(3)の文章を読んで、後の問いに対する答えとして最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。
- 問題12: 次のAとBはそれぞれ、〈topic〉について書かれた文章である。二つの文章を読んで、後の問いに対する答えとして最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。
- 問題13: 次の文章を読んで、後の問いに対する答えとして最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。
- 問題14: 右のページは、〈what it is〉である。下の問いに対する答えとして最もよいものを、1・2・3・4から一つ選びなさい。

## What each type looks like

**短文 (180–240 characters).** Essay extracts (an opinion on reading, on what
makes a painting), or practical texts with a lead-in line (以下は、ある会社が出したメールの
内容である。) such as a business email in full keigo (お客様各位 … 弊社では … 幸いです).
Questions: 筆者の考えに合うものはどれか / 〜について正しいものはどれか /
<u>下線部</u>とあるが、なぜか / 筆者が考える〜とはどのようなものか.

**中文 (430–600 characters).** One 評論 (a column with a claim), one 解説 (explains how or
why something works: fast running is hard to learn because the nerve circuits are already
fixed) and one エッセイ (the writer's experience leading to a view). Underlined parts are numbered ①②.
Typical question set: 1 × why ① (理由), 1 × what ② refers to (この場合とは
どんな場合か: 指示語), 1 × the writer's conclusion (筆者によると、〜にはどうすればよいか).
Sometimes one question is 〜の例として近いものはどれか: 4 new everyday cases; tag it 詳細.

**統合理解 (two texts of 250–290 characters each).** A and B on the same topic (the
future of cars), both reasonable, partly overlapping. Questions: AとBのどちらの文章にも
触れられている点は何か / AとBの筆者は、〜についてどのように考えているか. Choices for
the second are built as AもBも… / AもBも… / Aは…、Bは… / Aは…、Bは….

**主張理解 (780–900 characters).** A researcher's essay that argues an abstract claim
(why likes and dislikes shouldn't drive research). The writer's own experience is only
supporting evidence, not the body of the text. Questions:
why the writer holds the underlined view / a fact about the writer's experience /
筆者は、〜とはどのようなものだと考えているか (the main claim).

**情報検索 (a flyer, plan table or flowchart).** Two companies' moving plans: a table
with conditions (box count, who packs, price, notes like 「プラン④のみ、他のプランに
追加してのご利用が可能です」) and a yes/no flowchart. Each question is a person with 2–3
needs (10+ boxes, cheapest, but 5 urgent boxes fast); the answer combines plans and
depends on a footnote. The sample's questions need no arithmetic: they pick a plan from
conditions and a footnote. At most one question per passage needs a price calculation.

## How the wrong choices are built

From the sample's answer key:

- **Uses the text's words but changes one detail**: who, when, how much (5%引き vs
  5%引きより安く; プリンターを買う人 vs プリンターを使っている人).
- **Opposite of the text** (目の前にあるものをできるだけ現実に近づけて when the writer
  says copying what you see is never painting).
- **Adds something the text never says** (想像力で補い).
- **True in the text but not the answer to this question** (a reason given elsewhere, or
  the writer's example instead of the claim).
- **Over-general** (どんな研究であっても…).
- 情報検索: a plan that fits 2 of 3 needs, or misses the footnote.

Hard words get a gloss under the text: （注）購入する：買う. Choices never quote a long
stretch of the text verbatim; the right one paraphrases.

## Difficulty: measured from the official sample

Measured line by line on the sample pages (40 characters a line) in a full review on
2026-11-24.

| Type | Characters | Kanji | （注） glosses | Source of the real text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 短文 | about 215–225 | about 20–28% | 1–3 | essay extracts, a business email |
| 中文 | about 445–580 | about 24–35% | 5 each | a newspaper column (日本経済新聞), a popular-science book |
| 統合理解 | about 270 each | about 35–39% | 1 for the pair | two short opinion pieces |
| 主張理解 | about 825 | about 29% | 5 | a book by a researcher (robotics, on likes and dislikes) |

**Where the answer is.** It's often far from the underline:
- 短文(4): the underline is the first sentence ("人に強い影響を与えるのは大部からなる作品とは限りません"),
  but the reason in the answer (読み手の姿勢で決まる) comes from the **last** sentence
  (学ぼうとする姿勢があれば…).
- 主張理解: the main claim is in the **last two paragraphs**, after a pivot question
  (ところで、そもそも好き嫌いとは何だろうか？). The first half is a lead-in that a reader can
  mistake for the claim.

**How far the right answer is paraphrased.** It keeps one anchor word and rewords the rest;
about 1 in 5 shares none:
- 単なる偶然の積み重なりの結果なのだ → たまたまそうなったから
- 目の前の自然を心のなかに消化し、それをもう一度吐きだす → 目で見たものを心のなかに感じ取って描き表したもの
- 納得し、正当化するため（注: 間違っていなかったと思う） → 自分の行動や選択が間違っていなかったと思うために用いるもの

The **wrong** choices are the ones that reuse the text's words.

**The writing itself:**
- About half the claims are hedged: 〜のではないか, 〜と言えるのではないか, 〜のだろう, 〜のかもしれない, 〜ように思う.
  The rest state it plainly (〜のだ, 〜が大切だ). The reader has to tell the writer's view
  from the views the writer reports.
- Rhetorical questions carry the argument (なぜ安いのか。本来の価値は損なわれていないか。).
- Newspaper style uses short noun-ending sentences (不ぞろいな野菜は、むしろ手作り品を思わせる長所。).
- Vocabulary mixes abstract 漢語 with plain verbs. The 主張理解 sample has about 10 abstract
  漢語 in 790 characters (視点, 同様, 偶然, 選択, 後悔, 納得, 無意識, 元来, 正当化), and about
  10 kanji compounds per 100 characters overall. It writes 繰り返す, 避ける, 分かる, not
  反復する, 回避する, 判明する.
- N1-level words are glossed: 肥えている, ぬくもり, 感性, 規格, 粗悪品, 矯正, 糸口, 狭める,
  得手不得手, 元来, 正当化, 普遍的, 理屈. N2 words (揺らぐ, 言い切る) are never glossed.
- Essays are 20–39% kanji, about 29% on average; 統合理解 runs highest, 短文 lowest.
- Sentences average about 40 characters in 短文 and 中文, and 45–55 in 統合理解 and
  主張理解, with some long ones built on 〜が、〜し、〜ため.
- Formal patterns are rare. Of 〜ざるを得ない, 〜にほかならない, 〜に伴い, 〜をめぐって,
  〜にもかかわらず, 〜どころか, 〜とは限らない and 〜に過ぎない, the sample uses only
  〜とは限らない, once. The difficulty is in the argument, not in showcase grammar.

## Checklist before showing a passage

Every passage must pass all of these, or be rewritten:

1. Length and glosses within the ranges above.
2. 中文 and 主張理解: the writer's view is in the second half; 主張理解 has a pivot before
   the claim. 短文: the view comes first and is then supported in about half, at the end in
   the rest. Hedge the final claim (〜のではないか, 〜だろう, 〜かもしれない) in about half of
   passages; state it plainly (〜のだ, 〜が大切だ) in the rest.
3. At least one question per 中文/主張理解 passage needs a sentence **two or more
   sentences away** from the underline or question topic.
4. The right answer keeps one or two content words of the sentence that proves it and
   rewords the rest (sample: 15 of 19 do). Wrong answers reuse the text's words at least as
   much, so a shared word never gives the answer away.
5. Every wrong answer has a named trap (changed detail, opposite, not in text, true but
   not the answer, over-general, missed condition), and none is defensible.
6. 短文 has 1–3 （注）; 中文 and 主張理解 have 4–5 (the sample has 5 in each), each an N1-level word. No gloss word
   appears in a choice.
7. 情報検索: each question needs 2–3 conditions and at least one footnote or exception.
   At most one question per passage needs a price calculation.
8. Words: each 中文/主張理解 uses about 8–10 abstract 漢語 (傾向, 前提, 懸念, 要因, 観点,
   側面, 一概に, 本質, 根拠, 実態…), each 短文 about 3. Use plain verbs where a columnist
   would (繰り返す, 避ける, 増える, 分かる), not stiff 漢語 (反復, 回避, 増加する, 判明).
   Kanji share as in the sample: 中文 and 主張理解 about 30%, 統合理解 about 35%, 短文 20–28%.
   24–39% is the allowed range, not the target. Read it aloud: it must sound like a newspaper column.
9. Grammar: 0–2 formal N2 patterns per passage, only where natural. Sentence length as
   in "The writing itself" above.
10. Wrong choices: per question, at least 1 is changed detail, true but not the answer or
    missed condition. At most 2 are opposite or not in text, and at most 1 over-general. An
    opposite or not-in-text choice still uses the text's words or topic.
    No すべて, どれも, 一切, 決して or 必ず in a wrong choice unless the text invites it: these
    words give the choice away. No choice can be ruled out without reading the text.

## What this means when writing passages

- Written Japanese as a newspaper column or a book by a specialist: mostly だ・である,
  with about 1 短文 in 5 in です・ます, as in the sample;
  abstract argument, natural word choice. Full keigo for business emails and notices.
  Match the exam: not easier, and not stiffer. The difficulty comes from the argument,
  the paraphrase distance and close wrong choices, not from piling up 漢語.
- Genres: 主張理解 is a 評論. The 3 中文 are one 評論 (a column with a claim), one 解説
  (explains how or why something works) and one エッセイ (the writer's experience leading
  to a view), as in the sample. Topics: society, science, language, work, education.
- Gloss words above N2 with （注）, as the real test does.
- Each question has exactly one choice that the text supports; for every wrong choice,
  know which trap above it uses (needed for the explanation).
- Spread answers over 1–4.
